Popular Destinations

Discover Tiruvallur's Treasures

From historic temples and reservoirs to serene coastal lagons and bird sanctuaries — explore the best of Tiruvallur District.

Nature

Poondi Reservoir

Poondi, Tiruvallur District

Major freshwater reservoir and historic dam supplying water to Chennai. Famous for scenic water releases, surrounding greenery, and peaceful sunsets.

4.8
Nature

Pulicat Lake

Pulicat, Tiruvallur District

India's second largest brackish water lagoon. A haven for thousands of Greater Flamingos and migratory birds with serene boating experiences.

4.9
Wildlife

Pulicat Bird Sanctuary

Pulicat, Tiruvallur District

World-renowned bird sanctuary protecting flamingos, pelicans, herons, and rare migratory aquatic birds. Ideal for nature photography.

4.8
Temples

Sri Veeraraghava Perumal Temple

Tiruvallur Town Center

Ancient Divya Desam temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Features grand Dravidian architecture, sacred tank (Hruthtapanasini), and healing divine blessings.

5.0
Temples

Tiruttani Murugan Temple

Tiruttani Hilltop, Tiruvallur

One of the six sacred abodes of Lord Murugan, perched atop a scenic hill with 365 steps. Offers panoramic views, spiritual serenity, and grand festival celebrations.

4.9
Beaches

Pazhaverkadu Coastal Beach

Pazhaverkadu (Pulicat Coast)

Pristine golden beach along the Bay of Bengal featuring Dutch historical landmarks, traditional fishing boats, and relaxing sea breeze.

4.7
Temples

Sri Panchamukha Anjaneyaswami Temple

Tiruvallur District

Home to the towering 5-faced golden Lord Hanuman idol standing tall in divine majesty, attracting devotees across South India.

5.0
